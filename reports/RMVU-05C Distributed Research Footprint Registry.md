## Measurement — Pilot KPI (30–45 days)

### Site-side (GA4)

1. Referral sessions (`utm_campaign=rmvu05c`)
2. Organic impressions for canonical articles
3. Branded search change (manual GSC)
4. `insight_cta_report`
5. `report_start`
6. `report_form_complete`
7. `report_checkout_start`
8. `purchase_verified`

### External asset-specific

- Google Docs: page views (if available)
- LinkedIn: impressions / clicks
- PDF: downloads / link clicks

### Success indicators (non-guaranteed)

- Canonical article impressions increase
- Referral traffic occurs
- External asset indexed/discovered
- Company Report CTA interaction
- Branded searches increase
- AI citation/referral where measurable

**Do NOT promise ranking improvement.**
